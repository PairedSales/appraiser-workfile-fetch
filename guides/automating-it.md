# Automating it

Ask in plain words. The AI sets up the schedule, works out the rest, and walks you through anything it needs from you.

> Every night, fetch the data for my new orders from my email.

> Every night, fetch any address in my orders spreadsheet that does not have a folder yet.

> What is still missing?

What only you can do:

* Leave the PC awake at that hour, with Chrome open and signed in to connectMLS.
* Watch the first run and allow what it asks for, so nothing has to ask overnight.

Want order documents too, like engagement letters from your order emails? Ask for that the same way. It is not built in, so the AI builds the steps for your AMC portals with you watching the first few runs. Keep that in your own copy, since it names your clients.
